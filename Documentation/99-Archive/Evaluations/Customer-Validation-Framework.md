## 1. Interview Frameworks

### 1.1 Existing POD Artist (5-10 interviews planned)

**Target Profile:**
- Currently using Printful, Redbubble, Printify, or SPOD
- Making 10-20% margins
- Frustrated with quality or complexity
- 1-5 years of merch experience

**Interview Questions:**

**Opening:**
"Tell me about your current merch situation. What platform are you using, and how long have you been selling?"

**Pain Point Deep Dive:**
"Walk me through a typical merch order from your perspective. What's the most frustrating part of that process?"

**Feature Prioritization:**
"Rank these from 1 (most important) to 5 (least important):"
1. [ ] Higher margins
2. [ ] Better quality/feel of products
3. [ ] AI design assistance
4. [ ] Faster production/shipping
5. [ ] Better customer support for fans

**Switch Triggers:**
"What would make you switch from your current platform to a new one? Be specific."

**Pricing Sensitivity:**
"If a platform offered you 40-60% margins (vs. your current 10-20%) but charged a €2 premium on base cost to maintain quality, would you pay it?"

**Expected Responses to Capture:**
- Specific pain points with current platform
- Margin target thresholds
- Quality complaints from fans
- Feature desires (AI design, bulk orders, etc.)

### 1.2 Touring Band (3-5 interviews planned)

**Target Profile:**
- Active touring schedule
- Selling merch at shows and online
- Need bulk orders (50+ units)
- Sensitive to production turnaround time

**Interview Questions:**

**Opening:**
"How does merch work into your tour cycle? Do you have stock on the bus, or order per show?"

**Pain Point Deep Dive:**
"What's your biggest merch pain point while on tour?"

**Speed Requirements:**
"How fast do you need production turnaround when you're between shows?"

**Quality vs. Speed Tradeoff:**
"Would you accept a slightly higher cost for guaranteed 3-5 day production and premium quality, or do you prioritize lowest cost?"

**Current Fulfillment:**
"What happens when a fan has an issue with an order while you're on tour?"

**Bulk Order Experience:**
"Walk me through your last bulk order experience. What went well? What went wrong?"

**Expected Responses to Capture:**
- Turnaround time requirements (3-5 days ideal, 7+ days unacceptable)
- Bulk order pain points (MOQs, inconsistent quality, shipping delays)
- Fan complaint types received
- Premium quality vs. lowest price preference

---

### 1.3 Digital Artist / First-Time (5-10 interviews planned)

**Target Profile:**
- 5k-100k Instagram/social following
- No existing merch or minimal experience
- Zero budget for upfront costs
- Doesn't know print requirements (CMYK, DPI, bleed)

**Interview Questions:**

**Opening:**
"What stops you from selling merch right now?"

**Knowledge Gap:**
"How much do you know about print requirements? Have you prepared files for screen printing before?"

**AI Value Proposition:**
"If an AI assistant could help you with: (a) design creation, (b) file preparation for print, (c) mockup generation, which would be most valuable?"

**Cost Sensitivity:**
"What's your budget for launching merch, if any? Would you pay €0 upfront if you could use AI to create everything?"

**Platform Complexity:**
"What frustrates you most about existing platforms like Printful? Is it the dashboard, the technical requirements, or something else?"

**Expected Responses to Capture:**
- Barriers to entry (design skills, print knowledge, upfront costs)
- AI feature preferences
- Platform UX friction points
- Zero-upfront-cost appeal

---

### 1.4 Ethical Brand (3-5 interviews planned)

**Target Profile:**
- Vegan/fair-trade aligned brand values
- Willing to pay premium for ethical production
- Communicates ethics to customers
- May have existing ethical certification (B-Corp, Fair Trade, etc.)

**Interview Questions:**

**Values Alignment:**
"How important is ethical production to your brand? What does that mean to you?"

**Current Sourcing:**
"How do you currently communicate ethical sourcing to your customers?"

**Premium Willingness:**
"Would you pay 10-20% more for certified vegan/fair-trade production and equal wages?"

**Certification Importance:**
"Do you have or are you pursuing B-Corp, Fair Trade, or other certifications? How important is third-party verification?"

**Fan Feedback:**
"Have your fans asked about where/how your products are made? What do they care about most?"

**Expected Responses to Capture:**
- Premium willing to pay (€5-10 per unit)
- Certification importance
- Fan awareness and questions
- Current ethical communication methods

## 3. Waitlist Survey Framework

### 3.1 Post-Interview Automated Survey

**Trigger:** Sent 24-48 hours after voice interview completion

**Email Subject:** "One more thing about your Blackout Merchandise application"

**Survey Questions:**

1. **Pain Point Quantification:**
   "On a scale of 1-10, how painful is your current merch situation? (1 = not at all, 10 = extremely painful)"

2. **Current Platform:**
   "What platform do you use for merch right now? (Printful / Redbubble / Printify / SPOD / Other / None)"

3. **Margin Benchmarking:**
   "What are your current artist margins per sale? Please estimate: (<10% / 10-20% / 20-30% / 30-40% / 40%+)"

4. **Ethical Priority:**
   "On a scale of 1-10, how important is ethical/fair-trade/vegan production to you? (1 = not important, 10 = extremely important)"

5. **Feature Interest:**
   "Which of these features would make you switch to a new platform? Rank top 3:"
   - [ ] AI design assistance
   - [ ] Higher quality blanks (premium cotton)
   - [ ] Faster production/shipping
   - [ ] Better customer support
   - [ ] Bulk order capabilities
   - [ ] Zero upfront costs

6. **Demo Willingness:**
   "Would you be willing to try a demo of Blackout Merchandise in February if invited? (Yes / No / Maybe)"

**Data Capture:**
Store responses in `waiting_list_surveys` table with:
- `waitlist_id` (links to interview)
- `pain_score` (1-10)
- `current_platform`
- `current_margins`
- `ethical_priority` (1-10)
- `top_features` (array)
- `demo_interest` (boolean)

## 4. Validation Criteria for Product-Market Fit

### 4.1 Signal Thresholds

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| **Pain Score ≥ 7** | 7+ out of 10 on survey | Prioritize for beta access |
| **Willingness to Pay Premium** | ≥10% margin + €2-3 base cost premium | Strong ICP fit |
| **Ethical Priority ≥ 8** | 8+ out of 10 on survey | Target for marketing materials |
| **Demo Interest = Yes** | Explicit yes to trial | Invite to immediate beta |
| **Touring Band Need** | <7 day turnaround required | Fast-track production SOP |
| **AI Design Ranked #1-2** | AI assistance in top 2 priorities | Validate core value prop |

### 4.2 Red Flag Indicators

| Indicator | Threshold | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **Pain Score ≤ 4** | <4 out of 10 on survey | Nurture, don't prioritize |
| **Margin Expectation < 20%** | Unwilling to accept better margins | Wrong ICP, educate or exclude |
| **Demo Interest = No** | Unwilling to try beta | Nurture with content first |
| **Current Platform Satisfaction ≥ 8** | 8+ out of 10 happy with current | Hard conversion target |

## 5. Interview Execution Plan

### 5.1 Timeline

| Phase | Target | Timing | Goal |
|--------|----------|--------|-------|
| **Waitlist Phase** | 5-10 artists | Feb 2026 |
| **Crowdfunding Phase** | 3-5 bands | Mar 2026 |
| **Beta Phase** | 5-10 artists | Apr 2026 |

### 5.2 Interview Protocol

**Before Each Interview:**
1. Research artist's current merch situation (Instagram, website)
2. Prepare specific pain point questions based on platform usage
3. Review PRD Cat-Suite roles to reference relevant features

**During Interview:**
1. Record with permission (audio or notes)
2. Ask open-ended questions first, then specific follow-ups
3. Capture exact quotes for investor materials
4. Confirm demographic (platform used, years experience, audience size)

**After Interview:**
1. Transcribe key quotes within 24 hours
2. Update this document with collected quotes
3. Add to PRD Section 1.13.1 with attribution
4. Score based on validation criteria table
5. Tag in CRM (if available) with ICP fit score
