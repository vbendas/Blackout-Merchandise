## THE PROBLEM: THE SYSTEM IS BROKEN

### Pillar 1: Streaming Doesn't Pay

The music industry loves to talk about "supporting artists." The numbers tell a different story:

- **61%** of artists earn less than $10,000/year from streaming royalties (Spotify / MIDiA Research)
- To earn minimum wage from Spotify alone, an artist needs **350,000 streams per month**, every month
- One t-shirt sale at $35 generates the equivalent revenue of **8,750 Spotify streams**
- The average artist makes **8x more** per show in merch sales than from a year's worth of streaming

> *"Merchandise has evolved from an ancillary revenue stream into a primary commercial pillar."*
> — Creator Merchandise Market Report, 2026

### Pillar 2: Platforms Extract, Not Enable

The "easy" platforms aren't easy on your wallet:

| Platform | Artist Keeps | On a $30 T-Shirt |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Society6** | 5% | **$1.50** |
| **Redbubble (Standard)** | ~10% net | **~$3.00** |
| **Zazzle** | ~7% net after fees | **~$2.10** |
| **Amazon Merch** | 6-12% royalty | **$1.80-$3.60** |
| **Merch From** | **50%+** | **$15.00+** |

- **Society6** purged the majority of its artists in 2025 and fixed royalties at 5% (Michael Essek)
- **Redbubble** imposed a 50% platform fee on Standard accounts, plus excess markup fees (Redbubble Blog)
- **Zazzle** introduced a 35-50% Marketing Royalty Fee, causing a documented 29% decline in average earnings per sale (Zazzle Community)
- **65% of creators earn less than $100/year across all platforms** (Precedence Research)

### Pillar 3: Quality & Ethics Are Afterthoughts

The fast-fashion approach to merch is destroying the planet and your brand:

- $467 billion global counterfeit trade (OECD/EUIPO, 2021)
- Fashion industry: ~10% of global CO2 emissions
- 92 million tons of textile waste annually
- 2,700 liters of water per conventional cotton t-shirt
- EU will **ban generic "eco-friendly" labels** starting September 2026. No more greenwashing.

> *"Gen Z pays more for demonstrated, verifiable sustainability tied to artists they trust, not for generic 'green' labels."*
> — Market Research, 2026

### Pillar 4: Design Skills Are a Barrier

65% of creators are casual sellers (1-3 hours/week). Design tool subscriptions cost $55-100/month. Only 5% operate full-time. The barrier isn't creativity — it's **tools and infrastructure**.
