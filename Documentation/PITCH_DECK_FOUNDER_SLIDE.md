## Speaker Notes

### Opening (15 seconds)
*"Most founders ask for money to figure out if their idea works. I spent 12 months and €110K proving it will work."*

### The Decision (30 seconds)
*"After 9 years running Blackout Merchandise with clients like Meta and Google, I recognized the model's ceiling—€200K revenue cap, unsustainable customer acquisition, divided attention. Rather than waste investor capital figuring out the transition, I self-funded a systematic 12-month preparation."*

### The Execution (45 seconds)
*"Three strategic moves: First, relocated €110K in equipment to Portugal—40% cost reduction plus proximity to Europe's best organic blank suppliers. Second, became an AI professional—data annotation specialist, AI Quality Specialist at Spider.cloud, sold AI system to Chiliz. Third, optimized my burn rate by building a custom mobile workspace—extended runway from 4 months to 18 months, saving €15K annually."*

### The Proof (15 seconds)
*"This isn't a pitch from someone who needs rescue. This is an invitation to join a systematically de-risked opportunity. I've already proven I can execute under capital constraints. That's the discipline I'll bring as CEO."*

### The Close (5 seconds)
*"€110K invested. Expert committed. AI proven. Ready Day 1. Let's scale this together."*

**Total: 110 seconds (under 2 minutes)**

## Q&A Preparation

### Q: "Living in a van seems risky for a CEO role"
**A:** *"It's not risky—it's strategic. I extended my runway 4.5x without diluting equity or taking debt. Bezos built Amazon in a garage. Jobs lived in his car. Musk slept in his office. I optimized my burn rate to prove this works before asking others to invest. That's exactly the capital discipline I'll bring as CEO."*

### Q: "What if you need to relocate to Portugal quickly?"
**A:** *"I'm already mobile. My equipment is in Portugal. Expert is ready. I can relocate in 48 hours. The van isn't a constraint—it's the reason I have 18 months of runway instead of 4. When we close funding, I move Day 1."*

### Q: "Why should we trust you with our capital?"
**A:** *"Because I already proved I can build this with my own capital. I invested €110K, closed a profitable business, and systematically prepared for 12 months before asking for investment. Most founders ask you to bet on potential. I'm asking you to scale proven execution."*

## Slide Placement in Deck

**Option 1: Early (Slide 4-5)**
- Establishes credibility immediately
- Shows you're different from typical founders
- Hooks attention for rest of pitch

**Option 2: After Problem/Solution (Slide 8-9)**
- Natural flow: "Here's the problem, here's the solution, here's why I'm the one to execute"
- Reinforces capability after establishing need

**Option 3: Before "The Ask" (Slide 12-13)**
- Final credibility boost before funding request
- Answers unspoken question: "Why should we bet on you?"

**Recommended:** Option 1 (Early)
- Differentiates you immediately
- Creates memorable narrative thread
- Investors remember: "The founder who lives in a van" (in a good way)

## Content Customization by Audience

### For Tech Investors
**Emphasis:** AI credentials, corporate validation (Chiliz), systematic approach
**De-emphasize:** Van lifestyle (mention briefly as "burn rate optimization")

### For Impact Investors
**Emphasis:** Ethical mission, personal sacrifice, commitment to values
**Highlight:** Van lifestyle as aligned with sustainable/ethical brand values

### For Crowdfunding
**Emphasis:** Personal story, underdog narrative, total commitment
**Highlight:** Van lifestyle as relatable struggle and inspiring determination
