# The Merch From Story: Built in a Parking Lot

## The Decision

**November 2024. Dublin, Ireland.**

I was running a successful screen printing business. Nine years. Three countries. Clients like Meta, Google, Electric Picnic. €110,000 in equipment I'd built up debt-free.

But I had a problem.

I was working 60+ hours a week, spending all my energy acquiring customers, and hitting a revenue ceiling. I could see the future clearly—AI + ethical manufacturing + zero upfront costs for artists—but I couldn't build it while running a print shop.

So I made a decision most people thought was insane.

I closed my profitable business.

---

## The Plan

I didn't close it blindly. I had a plan—three moves that would change everything:

### **Move 1: Bet Everything on Portugal**
I shipped all €110,000 of equipment to Portugal. Every press, every printer, every screen. It was my bet on the future—40% lower costs, proximity to Europe's best organic blank suppliers (WATC-Studio in Barcelos), and the perfect base for an ethical merch empire.

### **Move 2: Become an AI Expert**
I didn't just "learn about AI." I spent an entire year becoming an AI professional:
- Got hired as a Data Annotation Analyst (Outlier.ai, Micro1.ai, Telus Digital)
- Enrolled in an AI & Automation MBA (Rocketseat)
- Advanced to AI Quality Specialist at Spider.cloud
- Built and sold an AI system to a major corporation (Chiliz/Socios.com)

I wanted to be an AI professional building an AI company—not another entrepreneur pretending to understand technology they don't.

### **Move 3: The Crazy Part**
I bought a 1993 Mercedes 308D van.

And I spent 30 days transforming it into a complete mobile home and office:
- Solar panels for off-grid power
- High-speed internet (Starlink) for remote work
- Full kitchen (oven, fridge, stove)
- Bathroom with shower and toilet
- Climate control (heater + AC)
- Ergonomic workspace
- Room for my two cats (they're family)

I built it all myself. Every panel, every wire, every fixture.

**Why?**

Because Dublin rent was €1,200/month. Van life? €360/month.

I just extended my runway from 4 months to 18 months without taking a single euro from investors.

---

## The Reality

**This is my office now.**

I wake up in parking lots around Dublin. Sometimes by the sea. Sometimes in industrial areas. Sometimes in quiet neighborhoods.

My cats sleep on the dashboard while I code. I watch Dublin sunrises through my windshield while planning Expert's production workflow. I design AI algorithms in Costa coffee shops when I need a change of scenery.

**This entire platform?** Designed in parking lots.

**The AI system?** Trained while my cats napped beside my laptop.

**The business plan?** Written watching rain on my van windows.

**The investor pitch?** Refined in a Tesco parking lot at midnight.

People thought I was crazy. My family was worried. Friends said I should "get a real job."

But I knew three things:

1. **Artists deserve better.** The current merch vendors charge too much, deliver low quality, and ignore ethics. Artists deserve premium, sustainable, affordable merch with zero upfront costs.

2. **AI + screen printing = billion-dollar opportunity.** Nobody is building screen-print-optimized AI with expert training data. We're going to own this space.

3. **If I don't bet everything on myself, why should anyone else?**

So here I am. Living in a van. Working 12-hour days. Learning AI. Building partnerships. Designing systems. Proving this works.
