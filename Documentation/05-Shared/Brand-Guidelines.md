## Brand Identity

### Brand Name
**Merch From** (stylized with space, no hyphen)

### Brand Taglines

**Primary (Aggressive):**
> *"Your fans wear your art. Why dress them in garbage?"*

**Alternative Taglines:**
- *"We don't work with everyone. But if you care about craft, we want to talk."*
- *"Premium Ethical Merch for Artists Who Give a Damn."*
- *"Screen Printing Meets AI. Artists Keep the Profits."*
- *"Buy Less, Buy Better."*
- *"If you know, you know."*

### Mission
Help artists, brands, and companies design, source, and sell premium quality merchandise through an AI-managed platform with professional visual tools.

### Core Values
- **Vegan & Fair-Trade**: Only ethical, sustainable products
- **Premium Quality**: No cheap merchandise
- **Artist-Centric**: Fair payouts, creative control
- **Zero Commission**: Direct artist-to-fan model
- **In-House Production**: Quality control over entire process

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics

> **Tone:** Aggressive, bold, unapologetic. We don't beg for customers — we curate them.

**Core Traits:**
- **Confident**: We know our value
- **Direct**: No fluff, no corporate speak
- **Exclusive**: "Velvet rope" energy
- **Authentic**: Raw, honest communication
- **Protective**: Fiercely loyal to our creators

### The "Velvet Rope" Philosophy

**Invite-only platforms don't explain themselves. Luxury brands don't beg for customers.**

Our public homepage should create **intrigue and exclusivity**, not marketing conversion:
- "If you know, you know" energy
- Portfolio showcase, not feature list
- Let the work speak for itself

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Landing page | Exclusive, intriguing | "We help artists produce premium merchandise. If you know, you know." |
| Onboarding | Warm, encouraging | "Welcome to the clowder. Let's build your empire." |
| Error messages | Helpful, character-driven | "Leopoldo says: Hmm, that email looks wrong. Try again?" |
| Success messages | Celebratory | "Your store is LIVE. Go tell your fans!" |
| Support | Empathetic, efficient | "Antonia's on it. She's tracking your order now." |

## Visual Identity

### Logo Usage

**Full Logo**
- Use on dark backgrounds (black, dark gray)
- Minimum size: 120px wide
- Clear space: 1/4 of logo width

**Color Variants:**
- Primary: Gold gradient (#D4AF37 → #F5E6A3)
- Secondary: White (#FFFFFF)
- No light mode version

### Color Philosophy

| Element | Value | Meaning |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Pure Black** | #000000 | Landing page — dramatic, luxury |
| **Zinc-950** | #0a0a0a | App backgrounds — professional |
| **Gold Sheen** | Gradient | Premium, exclusive, CTAs |
| **Recording Red** | #E11D48 | Action, urgency, voice active |
| **White** | #FFFFFF | Maximum contrast, primary text |

### Typography

| Use | Font | Weight | Personality |
|-----|------|--------|-------------|
| Headings | Space Grotesk | 600-700 | Quirky, tech-inspired |
| Body | Inter | 400-500 | Clean, invisible |

## Content Guidelines

### Do:
- Use character voices for in-app communication
- Keep landing page copy minimal and exclusive
- Lead with portfolio, not features
- Use "your" and "you" — direct address
- Embrace the aggressive, confident tone

### Don't:
- Use corporate jargon or buzzwords
- Explain every feature (let them discover)
- Beg for signups or purchases
- Use light mode anywhere
- Make the characters too cute or childish

### Writing Examples

**Landing Page Hero:**
```
❌ "Welcome to Merch From! We help you create amazing merchandise
    with our AI-powered platform. Sign up today!"

✅ "We help artists produce premium merchandise."
   [Portfolio grid below. No explanation needed.]
```

**Feature Introduction:**
```
❌ "Our platform utilizes AI algorithms for design assistance."

✅ "Francisca handles your designs. She's demanding,
    but your prints will be perfect."
```

**Error Message:**
```
❌ "Input validation failed."

✅ "Leopoldo says: That email looks suspicious, my friend. Try again?"
```
