# Merch Coins: Comprehensive Marketing Strategy

## Executive Summary

**Merch Coins** is a zero-cost marketing amplification system that transforms artists into your viral growth engine. Artists receive free monthly coin allocations (1 coin = €1 discount) to distribute via social media campaigns, with all discounts coming exclusively from their profit margins. This creates authentic, artist-driven customer acquisition at zero cost to the platform.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Platform Cost:** €0 (discounts from artist margin)
- **Expected Social Reach:** 50-100 impressions per coin distributed
- **Redemption Rate Target:** 40% of distributed coins
- **Customer Acquisition Cost:** €0 via organic artist campaigns
- **Artist ROI:** 200-400% return on coin distribution efforts

## System Overview

### Core Concept

Merch Coins are **platform-only promotional credits** that artists use as a marketing tool to grow their fanbase and drive sales. The system operates on three fundamental principles:

1. **Artist-Funded:** All coin discounts come from artist profit margins
2. **Zero Platform Cost:** Platform never absorbs coin discounts
3. **Social Amplification:** Artists leverage social media reach organically

### How It Works

```
Monthly Cycle:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1st of Month: Artist receives coins             │
│    - Free tier: 100 coins                       │
│    - Pro tier: 500 coins                        │
│    - Studio tier: 1,000 coins                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Artist runs social campaign                     │
│    "RT this + tag 3 friends = 5 Merch Coins!"   │
│    Distributes coins to winners                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Winners redeem coins at checkout                │
│    Customer: Saves €5                           │
│    Artist: Loses €5 from their margin           │
│    Platform: Production costs still covered     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
         ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Results:                                        │
│    - Artist gains followers + engagement        │
│    - Platform gains new customers               │
│    - Everyone wins (except spam/bots)           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Key Features

**For Artists:**
- Free monthly allocation based on subscription tier
- Full control over distribution (who, how many, when)
- ROI tracking dashboard
- Performance bonuses for milestones
- 6-month expiry encourages active use

**For Customers:**
- Receive coins via social media or direct from artists
- 1 coin = €1 off at checkout
- Works on any artist's store
- Expiry notifications create urgency

**For Platform:**
- Zero cost (discounts from artist margin)
- Organic customer acquisition
- Artist retention (coins = sunk cost if they leave)
- Viral social reach
- Built into subscription tiers (monetization)

## Economic Model

### Coin Allocations by Tier

| Tier | Monthly Cost | Monthly Coins | Effective Marketing Budget | Cost per Coin |
|------|--------------|---------------|----------------------------|---------------|
| **Free** | €0 | 100 | €100 discount power | €0 |
| **Pro** | $20 | 500 | €500 discount power | $0.04 |
| **Studio** | $50 | 1,000 | €1,000 discount power | $0.05 |

### Transaction Economics

**Example Order with Coins:**

```
Product Price: €25
Artist Margin: €10 (40%)
Platform Cost: €15 (production + base margin)

Customer uses 5 Merch Coins:
├─ Customer Pays: €20 (€25 - €5)
├─ Artist Gets: €5 (€10 margin - €5 coin discount)
└─ Platform Gets: €15 (unchanged)

Result:
✅ Platform: Full production cost covered + base margin
✅ Customer: €5 saved
✅ Artist: €5 margin remaining + marketing ROI
```

### Guardrails & Limits

**To Prevent Abuse:**

1. **Maximum Discount:** 50% of order value
   ```
   €30 order → Max 15 coins usable (€15 discount)
   €100 order → Max 50 coins usable (€50 discount)
   ```

2. **Minimum Order Value:** €10
   ```
   Prevents: Cheap item abuse (€5 item with 5 coins = free)
   Ensures: Meaningful purchases only
   ```

3. **Artist Margin Cap:**
   ```
   If artist margin = €8 on this product
   Customer can only use max 8 coins
   Prevents: Platform from absorbing discount
   ```

4. **Expiry:** 6 months rolling
   ```
   Coins received Feb 1 → Expire Aug 1
   Creates urgency without feeling unfair
   ```

5. **No Stacking:**
   ```
   Cannot combine: Merch Coins + Platform Promotion
   Customer chooses best discount
   ```

### Platform Promotions (Separate System)

**These are YOUR marketing costs, not artist-funded:**

| Promotion Type | Example | Who Absorbs | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|-------------|---------|
| New Customer | "€10 off first order" | Platform | Acquisition |
| Seasonal | "Black Friday: Buy 3 Pay 2" | Platform | Volume sales |
| Cart Abandon | "Come back for €5 off" | Platform | Recovery |
| AOV Boost | "Free shipping over €50" | Platform | Increase basket |
| Bundle | "Hoodie + Tee, save 15%" | Platform | Multi-item |

**Key Difference:**
- **Merch Coins:** Artist-driven, artist-funded, organic social reach
- **Platform Promos:** Platform-driven, platform-funded, direct acquisition

Channel: Paid ads, influencer partnerships
Target: New visitors

Goal: Lower barrier to first purchase

- Customer pays €20 for €30 product
- Platform still makes €5 margin (€15 cost - €10 discount)
- Acceptable loss leader for customer acquisition

-- Coin Balances
CREATE TABLE merch_coin_balances (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id UUID REFERENCES user_profiles(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  user_type TEXT CHECK (user_type IN ('artist', 'customer')),
  balance INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
  earned_lifetime INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
  spent_lifetime INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
  expires_soon INTEGER DEFAULT 0,  -- Count of coins expiring <30 days

-- Transaction Ledger
CREATE TABLE merch_coin_transactions (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  from_user_id UUID,  -- NULL for platform allocations
  to_user_id UUID NOT NULL,
  amount INTEGER NOT NULL,
  transaction_type TEXT NOT NULL,
  reference_id UUID,  -- Links to orders, campaigns, etc.
  description TEXT,
  expires_at TIMESTAMP,
  created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT NOW(),
  
  CHECK (amount != 0)
);

CREATE INDEX idx_coin_tx_to_user ON merch_coin_transactions(to_user_id);
CREATE INDEX idx_coin_tx_type ON merch_coin_transactions(transaction_type);
CREATE INDEX idx_coin_tx_expiry ON merch_coin_transactions(expires_at);

### Key Functions

#### Monthly Allocation (Cron Job)
```typescript
import cron from 'node-cron';

// Runs 1st of every month at 00:00 UTC
cron.schedule('0 0 1 * *', async () => {
  console.log('Running monthly coin allocation...');
  
  const artists = await db.query(`
    SELECT id, tier, email, name 
    FROM user_profiles 
    WHERE user_type = 'creator' 
      AND active = true
      AND tier IN ('free', 'pro', 'studio')
  `);
  
  const allocations = {
    free: 100,
    pro: 500,
    studio: 1000
  };
  
  for (const artist of artists.rows) {
    const coins = allocations[artist.tier];
    const expiresAt = new Date();
    expiresAt.setMonth(expiresAt.getMonth() + 6);
    
    // Add coins
    await db.query(`
      INSERT INTO merch_coin_transactions 
      (from_user_id, to_user_id, amount, transaction_type, description, expires_at)
      VALUES (NULL, $1, $2, 'monthly_allocation', $3, $4)
    `, [artist.id, coins, `${artist.tier} tier allocation`, expiresAt]);
    
    // Update balance
    await db.query(`
      UPDATE merch_coin_balances 
      SET balance = balance + $1, 
          earned_lifetime = earned_lifetime + $1,
          last_updated = NOW()
      WHERE user_id = $2
    `, [coins, artist.id]);
    
    // Send email notification
    await sendEmail(artist.email, {
      subject: `You've received ${coins} Merch Coins!`,
      body: `Your monthly allocation of ${coins} coins is ready. Use them to run campaigns and grow your fanbase!`
    });
  }
  
  console.log(`Allocated coins to ${artists.rows.length} artists`);
});
```

#### Coin Redemption at Checkout
```typescript
export async function applyMerchCoins(
  orderId: string, 
  customerId: string, 
  coinsToUse: number
): Promise<Order> {
  
  // 1. Get order details
  const order = await getOrder(orderId);
  if (order.customerId !== customerId) {
    throw new Error('Unauthorized');
  }
  
  // 2. Check customer balance
  const customer = await getCoinBalance(customerId);
  if (customer.balance < coinsToUse) {
    throw new Error(`Insufficient coins. You have ${customer.balance}, trying to use ${coinsToUse}`);
  }
  
  // 3. Calculate discount
  const coinDiscount = coinsToUse * 1.00; // 1 coin = €1
  
  // 4. Validate against guardrails
  const artistMargin = order.totalPrice - order.baseCost;
  
  if (coinDiscount > artistMargin) {
    throw new Error(`Coins exceed artist margin. Max ${artistMargin} coins for this order.`);
  }
  
  if (coinDiscount > order.totalPrice * 0.5) {
    throw new Error('Maximum 50% discount with coins');
  }
  
  if (order.totalPrice < 10) {
    throw new Error('Coins can only be used on orders over €10');
  }
  
  // 5. Apply discount
  const updatedOrder = await db.query(`
    UPDATE orders SET
      coin_discount = $1,
      artist_revenue = $2,
      customer_paid = $3,
      discount_type = 'merch_coins',
      updated_at = NOW()
    WHERE id = $4
    RETURNING *
  `, [
    coinDiscount,
    artistMargin - coinDiscount,
    order.totalPrice - coinDiscount,
    orderId
  ]);
  
  // 6. Deduct coins from customer
  await db.query(`
    INSERT INTO merch_coin_transactions
    (from_user_id, to_user_id, amount, transaction_type, reference_id, description)
    VALUES ($1, NULL, $2, 'redemption', $3, $4)
  `, [
    customerId,
    -coinsToUse,
    orderId,
    `Redeemed on order #${order.orderNumber}`
  ]);
  
  await db.query(`
    UPDATE merch_coin_balances 
    SET balance = balance - $1,
        spent_lifetime = spent_lifetime + $1,
        last_updated = NOW()
    WHERE user_id = $2
  `, [coinsToUse, customerId]);
  
  return updatedOrder.rows[0];
}
```

#### Expiry Cleanup (Daily Cron)
```typescript
// Runs daily at 02:00 UTC
cron.schedule('0 2 * * *', async () => {
  console.log('Running coin expiry cleanup...');
  
  // Find expired coins
  const expiredCoins = await db.query(`
    SELECT to_user_id, SUM(amount) as expired_amount
    FROM merch_coin_transactions
    WHERE expires_at < NOW()
      AND transaction_type NOT IN ('expiry', 'redemption')
    GROUP BY to_user_id
    HAVING SUM(amount) > 0
  `);
  
  for (const row of expiredCoins.rows) {
    // Deduct expired coins
    await db.query(`
      INSERT INTO merch_coin_transactions
      (from_user_id, to_user_id, amount, transaction_type, description)
      VALUES ($1, NULL, $2, 'expiry', 'Coins expired after 6 months')
    `, [row.to_user_id, -row.expired_amount]);
    
    await db.query(`
      UPDATE merch_coin_balances
      SET balance = balance - $1,
          last_updated = NOW()
      WHERE user_id = $2
    `, [row.expired_amount, row.to_user_id]);
    
    console.log(`Expired ${row.expired_amount} coins for user ${row.to_user_id}`);
  }
  
  // Send expiry warnings (30 days before)
  const soonToExpire = await db.query(`
    SELECT to_user_id, email, SUM(amount) as expiring_amount, MIN(expires_at) as first_expiry
    FROM merch_coin_transactions t
    JOIN user_profiles u ON t.to_user_id = u.id
    WHERE expires_at > NOW()
      AND expires_at < NOW() + INTERVAL '30 days'
      AND transaction_type NOT IN ('expiry', 'redemption')
    GROUP BY to_user_id, email
  `);
  
  for (const row of soonToExpire.rows) {
    await sendEmail(row.email, {
      subject: `${row.expiring_amount} Merch Coins expiring soon!`,
      body: `You have ${row.expiring_amount} coins expiring on ${row.first_expiry}. Use them before they're gone!`
    });
  }
});
```

## Risk Management

### Risk 1: Low Artist Adoption

**Risk:** Artists don't understand or use coins

**Mitigation:**
- Clear onboarding with examples
- Pre-made campaign templates
- Success stories from beta users
- Email drip campaign with tips
- "Coin Coach" support in dashboard

### Risk 2: Low Redemption Rates

**Risk:** Customers receive coins but don't redeem

**Mitigation:**
- Expiry notifications (30/15/7 days before)
- Email showcasing what they can buy
- Minimum order threshold is low (€10)
- Reminder: "You have €X off waiting!"

### Risk 3: Fraud/Abuse

**Risk:** Fake accounts, bot campaigns, self-dealing

**Mitigation:**
- Max 50% discount prevents $0 orders
- Minimum €10 order prevents abuse on cheap items
- Flag suspicious patterns (same IP, rapid redemptions)
- Require verified email to receive coins
- Manual review for high-value coin distributions

### Risk 4: Artist Misunderstanding Economics

**Risk:** Artists give too many coins, hurt their margins

**Mitigation:**
- Clear dashboard: "Giving 100 coins could cost you €100"
- Profit calculator: "If 40% redeem, you'll pay €40"
- Suggested allocations based on typical redemption
- Alerts: "You're giving away a lot - are you sure?"

### Risk 5: Platform Promotions Conflict

**Risk:** Customers confused about coin vs promo stacking

**Mitigation:**
- Clear messaging: "Choose best discount"
- Automatic calculation shows savings comparison
- FAQ: "Why can't I stack discounts?"
- Customer support training
