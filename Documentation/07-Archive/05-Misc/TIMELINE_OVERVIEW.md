# Merch From: Complete Timeline Overview

## Phase Summary

**Total Time from Start to Launch:** 7 months  
**Crowdfunding Campaign:** Weeks 9-12  
**Backer Coin Redemption:** Month 7 (7 months after campaign ends)

---

## Detailed Timeline

### **Phase 1: Pre-Crowdfunding Validation (Weeks 1-8)**

**Goal:** Build demo MVP for crowdfunding video and pitch deck

**Week 1-4: Demo MVP Build**
- 1 fully polished demo artist storefront
- 15-20 realistic product mockups (AI-generated)
- **Frontend showcase ONLY** — no backend, no real payment processing
- **No Merch Coins system yet** — just mention in pitch/video
- 90-second platform walkthrough video for campaign
- High-converting waitlist landing page

**Week 5-8: Waitlist Building**
- Reddit (r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, r/bandmembers, r/streetwearstartup)
- Facebook Groups (independent musician communities)
- Instagram/TikTok (partner with 3-5 micro-influencer artists)
- ProductHunt launch
- Cold outreach to 50 artists with 5k-50k followers

**Target:** 500+ waitlist signups, 70%+ prioritize ethical/sustainable products

**Note:** This is a demo/validation MVP only — NOT production-ready

---

### **Phase 2: Crowdfunding Campaign (Weeks 9-12)**

**Week 9: Campaign Prep**
- Finalize Kickstarter/Indiegogo campaign page
- Professional campaign video (founder story, mission, demo walkthrough)
- Reward tier graphics and descriptions
- Early Bird pricing strategy for first 48 hours
- Email sequence for waitlist → backer conversion

**Week 10-12: Live Campaign (30 days)**
- Target: €35K minimum, €50K stretch goal
- Daily social media updates + behind-the-scenes content
- Artist interviews and testimonials
- Weekly milestone celebrations
- Press outreach to ethical business + music media

**Success Metric:** €46K raised from 488 backers
- 250 Supporters (€25)
- 150 Artist Pioneers (€99)
- 50 Artist VIPs (€249)
- 5 Brand Partners (€999)
- 3 Sponsors (€2,500)

**Backer Communication:** 
- Tier confirmations sent
- "Your Platform Merch Coins will be redeemable when the platform launches (estimated Month 7)"

---

### **Phase 3: Post-Crowdfunding Setup (Months 1-3)**

**Month 1: Facility Activation in Portugal**
- Secure industrial unit (deposit + first month from crowdfunding capital)
- Equipment retrieval from Ireland storage → Portugal
- Facility improvements (electrical, ventilation, lighting)
- Equipment installation and testing of existing equipment

**Month 2: Initial Inventory + Production Testing**
- Order initial inventory (500-1,000 organic/fair-trade blanks)
- Test production runs (DTG, DTF, screen printing)
- Refine production workflow and quality standards
- Staff training on equipment

**Month 3: Investor Outreach**
- Prepare investor pitch deck with crowdfunding traction data
- Pitch investors with proof of demand (€46K raised, 150+ artist backers)
- Secure €230K investment (reduced from €280K due to crowdfunding capital)

**Status at End of Month 3:**
- ✅ Portugal facility operational
- ✅ Equipment installed and tested
- ✅ Inventory stocked
- ✅ Production workflow established
- ✅ €46K crowdfunding capital deployed
- ✅ €230K investor capital secured
- ❌ No full platform yet (demo only)
- ❌ No Merch Coins system yet
- ❌ No artist stores live yet

---

### **Phase 4: Post-Investment Build & Expansion (Months 4-6)**

**Month 4: Premium Equipment Expansion**
- Order Anatol automatic press + 2 new DTG printers (investor capital)
- Hire Production Manager (€1,400/month)
- Equipment delivery and installation begins

**Month 5-6: Full Platform Development + Equipment Setup**

**Complete production-ready platform build:**
- ✅ Conversational UI (AI-powered store management)
- ✅ Real payment processing (Stripe integration)
- ✅ **Merch Coins system (platform + artist coins)** — **CRITICAL FOR LAUNCH**
- ✅ Artist onboarding workflow
- ✅ Order management + fulfillment tracking
- ✅ Product catalog + mockup generation
- ✅ Email notifications and customer dashboard

**Parallel: Equipment Setup**
- Install and test new equipment (Anatol press, DTG printers)
- Establish production standards and quality control
- Production team training and workflow optimization

**Status at End of Month 6:**
- ✅ Full production-ready platform live
- ✅ Merch Coins system operational
- ✅ Premium equipment installed (Anatol press)
- ✅ Production capacity: 400-600 prints/hour
- ✅ Team trained and ready
- ✅ Ready for artist onboarding

---

### **Phase 5: Launch (Month 7)** 🚀

**Week 1-2: Artist Onboarding**
- Onboard first 10 artists (Artist Pioneer + VIP backers from crowdfunding)
- 1-on-1 store setup consultations
- Design consultation sessions (for VIP tier)
- Test real orders internally

**Week 3: Platform Launch**
- **Launch first real artist stores** (production-ready, real payment processing)
- **Credit all backer accounts with Platform Merch Coins:**
  - Supporters: 30 coins
  - Artist Pioneers: 100 coins
  - Artist VIPs: 250 coins
  - Brand Partners: 1,000 coins
  - Sponsors: 3,000 coins

**Week 4: First Orders Fulfilled**
- **Backers can NOW redeem coins** (7 months after crowdfunding campaign ended)
- Begin fulfilling real orders with operational production facility
- Monitor quality, customer satisfaction, and unit economics

---

### **Phase 6: Scale (Months 8-10)**

**Month 8: Second Artist Cohort**
- Onboard 20 more artists
- Hire Customer Success Manager (€1,400/month)
- Processing 50+ orders/week

**Month 9: Third Artist Cohort**
- Onboard 30 more artists (total 60 artists)
- Optimize production workflow based on real order data

**Month 10: Growth Phase**
- Hire Marketing Lead (€1,400/month)
- Fourth cohort: 40 more artists (total 100 artists)
- Open beta or continued invite-only based on capacity
- Evaluate metrics for next funding round or profitability path

## Merch Coins System: Development Timeline

### **Not Needed Yet:**
- ❌ Phase 1 (Weeks 1-8): Demo MVP — coins just mentioned
- ❌ Phase 2 (Weeks 9-12): Crowdfunding — coins promised, not delivered
- ❌ Phase 3 (Months 1-3): Facility setup — no platform build yet

### **Critical Development Period:**
- ✅ **Month 5-6:** Full Merch Coins system must be built
  - Database schema (platform + artist coins)
  - Coin balance display (customer wallet)
  - Checkout redemption flow
  - Validation logic (margin limits, artist-specific restrictions)
  - Manual coin issuance tool (to credit backers)
  - Email notifications

### **Go Live:**
- ✅ **Month 7:** Platform launches, coins credited to backer accounts
- ✅ Supporters can immediately redeem 30 coins
- ✅ Artist Pioneer/VIP backers onboarded as first artists

## Why This Timeline Works

✅ **Demo MVP keeps costs low** (frontend only, no backend complexity)  
✅ **Crowdfunding validates demand** before major investment  
✅ **Facility setup happens early** (uses crowdfunding capital)  
✅ **Investor pitch is stronger** (operational facility + community proof)  
✅ **Platform build happens post-investment** (proper funding, no rush)  
✅ **Merch Coins ready when needed** (Month 5-6 development, Month 7 launch)  
✅ **7-month wait is reasonable** for crowdfunding backers (clearly communicated)  
✅ **Launch is real** (not beta, not soft launch — production-ready from day one)  

---

*This timeline balances speed, cost efficiency, and quality. The demo MVP proves the concept, crowdfunding validates demand, and the full platform build happens with proper funding and time to do it right.*
