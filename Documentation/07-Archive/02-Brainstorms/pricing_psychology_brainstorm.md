# Pricing Psychology & Margin Analysis

**The Core Question:** Can we justify a €15 base cost when competitors are cheaper?
**The Thesis:** Yes, because we sell *Profit Dollars*, not *Profit Margins %*.

## 1. The "Profit per Transaction" Comparison
Investors (and smart artists) care about how much cash enters their bank, not percentages.

| Platform | Base Cost (Black Tee) | Rec. Retail Price | **Artist Keeps (Profit)** | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Merch From** | **€15.00** (Heavyweight) | **€30.00** | **€15.00** | **Premium Organic 220gsm** |
| Printful | €12.95 (Standard Gildan) | €25.00 | €12.05 | Basic 150gsm (Gildan 64000) |
| Redbubble | €18.00 (Base + Markups) | €22.00 | €4.00 | Artist has little control. Volume play. |
| Local Shop | €8.00 (Bulk 50+) | €30.00 | €22.00 | Requires upfront cash (€400+) + Risk. |

**The Insight:**
*   Merch From beats Printful on **Quality-Adjusted Profit**. To get a 220gsm tee on Printful, the base price jumps to ~€19.00, dropping artist profit to €6.00 (assuming same €25 retail) or forcing retail to €35+.
*   **We are the "Sweet Spot":** Better profit than POD, significantly better quality than POD, but zero risk compared to Bulk.

## 2. Marketing the "Premium" Justification
How do we teach artists to sell a €30 tee when they are used to €22 Redbubble trash?

### Argument A: "Don't sell Merch, Sell merch."
*   **Low Quality (Redbubble):** "I bought a shirt to support the band." (Charity).
*   **High Quality (Merch From):** "I bought a sick shirt that happens to be from the band." (Fashion).
*   **Data:** Return rates on cheap POD are ~5-7%. Return rates on premium streetwear are <2%.

### Argument B: The "Fan Lifetime Value"
*   If a fan buys a €20 shirt and it shrinks/fades, they never buy again. **LTV = €20.**
*   If a fan buys a €30 Merch From shirt and it lasts 3 years, they buy the hoodie next winter. **LTV = €100+.**
*   *Leopoldo's Line:* "Do you want a customer for today, or a customer for life?"

## 3. The Psychology of €30
*   €30 is the "gig standard." If an artist sells for less, fans subconsciously assume it's cheap/fake.
*   **Anchoring:** We anchor against "Streetwear Brands" (Stussy €50, Carhartt €45), not "Merch Sites."
*   **Visual Trust:** Our mockups (Video of ink deposit, A2 size) make €30 feel like a bargain.
