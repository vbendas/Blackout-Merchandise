## Key Strategic Messages

### Core Narrative
> "We're not just building a platform - we're building a real, tangible business with hard assets. Year 1-2: We rent to stay agile during validation. Year 2-3: We buy to build equity."

### Investor Value Proposition
1. **Risk Mitigation** - Real estate provides asset backing
2. **Value Creation** - Dual value streams (operations + property)
3. **Exit Optionality** - Property sellable separately or in acquisition
4. **Permanence Signal** - Long-term market commitment
5. **Competitive Moat** - Lower costs vs rent-dependent competitors
6. **Capital Discipline** - Rent first (speed), own later (control)

### Financial Impact
- €120K property purchase in Year 2
- €10-20K appreciation by Year 3 (Portugal industrial market trend)
- €14K/year rent savings post-purchase
- 5-10% exit valuation premium
- Total value creation: €40-60K by Year 3

## Financing Options Documented

### Option A: Self-Financed (Most Likely)
- €30K down payment from accumulated profits
- €90K bank mortgage (70-80% LTV standard in Portugal)
- 4-5% interest rate (SME lending rates)
- No equity dilution
- **Requirements:** €20-30K/month profit by Month 18

### Option B: Series A Inclusion
- Include €120K property in €500K+ Series A raise
- Appropriate if scaling faster than projected
- Equity dilution acceptable given larger round
- **Triggers:** Surpassing revenue targets, international expansion timing

### Option C: Sale-Leaseback (Creative)
- Investor owns property, leases to business
- Investor gets asset backing + rental income
- Business gets facility control without purchase
- **Use case:** Conservative investor wants downside protection
