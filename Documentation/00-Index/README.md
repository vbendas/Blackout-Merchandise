## The Project at a Glance

**Merch From** is an AI-powered premium merchandise platform for artists who care about quality.

### Key Differentiators
1. **Cat-Suite AI Agents** — Leopoldo, Francisca, Antonia guide the entire artist journey
2. **In-House Production** — Own facility in Portugal, €113K equipment
3. **Zero Risk Model** — Artists pay nothing upfront
4. **Ethical First** — Vegan, fair-trade, organic blanks as standard
5. **Proprietary Dataset** — World's only expert-scored screen print AI training data

### Investment Ask
| Investment | Equity | Runway |
|------------|--------|--------|
| €300,000 | 16-18% | 24 months to profitability |

### Market Opportunity
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| TAM | $340B (Global Licensed Merchandise) |
| SAM | $12B (European Creator Merch) |
| SOM (Year 1-3) | $50M (0.4% market capture) |
