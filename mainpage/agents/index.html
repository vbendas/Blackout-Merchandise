03

What your agent can do

Asks you firstWaits for your confirmation before it runs. These are the tools Merch Studio's agent uses today.

Catalog and blanks

02
  • list_blanksBrowse and search the blank catalogue by type, fit, colour or text.
  • get_blankFull specs for one blank: colours, sizes, size guide and print services.

Briefs and design

06
  • analyze_designChecks print readiness: resolution at a 28 cm print width, transparency and background.
  • clean_designRemoves a background, upscales or trims. Your original stays untouched.
  • edit_designChanges artwork from an instruction, as a new version beside the original.
  • compose_designBuilds a design around your logo. The logo is never altered.
  • generate_designMakes new artwork from a text brief and returns two variants.
  • list_style_capsulesLists the style presets you can use for new artwork.

Mockups and products

08
  • preview_on_blankTries a design on a blank, in one or more colours, before a product exists.
  • render_mockupsPuts your design on the blank for every enabled colour.
  • create_productCreates a draft: a design on a blank, with print service, placement, colours and sizes.
  • update_productChanges a product’s name, description, placement, colours, sizes or collection.
  • list_productsLists your products, optionally by status.
  • get_productFull details for one product: colours, sizes, price, margin and status.
  • create_collectionCreates a named collection to group products.
  • assign_to_collectionMoves a product into a collection, or out of one.

Quotes and proofs

11
  • get_quotePrices a run of a blank and print service at a quantity. Totals only.
  • set_priceSets your retail price and shows your margin.
  • submit_proofSends the current mockups and spec for approval.
  • request_proof_changesSends a proof back with notes.
  • approve_proofApproves a pending proof so the product is ready to publish.Asks you first
  • create_bulk_quoteQuotes a bulk run that ships to you, line by line, with colour and sizes.
  • submit_bulk_proofSends the bulk quote for approval.
  • request_bulk_changesSends a bulk proof back with notes.
  • approve_bulk_proofApproves the bulk proof so the order can be paid.Asks you first
  • pay_bulk_orderStarts checkout and returns a Stripe payment link. You pay there.
  • cancel_bulk_orderCancels a bulk order that has not been paid yet.Asks you first

Orders and production status

05
  • list_ordersLists your store’s orders, newest first, by status or date.
  • get_orderOne order: lines, totals, status and tracking.
  • get_sales_summaryOrder count, gross, top products and a daily series for a period.
  • list_bulk_ordersLists your bulk orders with status and totals.
  • get_bulk_orderOne bulk order: lines, pricing, status and ship-to address.

Store

12
  • get_my_storeYour store’s name, slug, status, payout readiness and live products.
  • update_storeChanges the display name, slug, accent colour, about text or socials.
  • start_stripe_onboardingStarts or resumes Stripe Connect setup and returns the link.
  • get_payout_statusWhether payouts are ready, and what is still missing.
  • publish_productPuts a product in the live store. Needs an approved proof.Asks you first
  • archive_productRemoves a product from the live store, or drops a draft.Asks you first
  • publish_storeOpens the store to fans. Needs Stripe payouts and one live product.Asks you first
  • unpublish_storeTakes the store offline.Asks you first
  • add_custom_domainConnects your own domain and returns the DNS records to add.
  • check_domainRe-checks a connected domain’s DNS and verification.
  • list_domainsLists your connected domains and whether each one is verified.
  • remove_domainDisconnects a custom domain.Asks you first

Support and inbox

08
  • contact_supportOpens a support ticket with your message.
  • get_ticketOpens one ticket with its full thread.
  • reply_ticketAdds a reply to one of your tickets.
  • get_inboxYour notifications: sales, proofs and system messages.
  • mark_inbox_readMarks notifications as read.
  • update_notification_prefsTurns Discord and email notifications on or off.
  • connect_discordShows whether Discord is linked, and how to link it.
  • disconnect_discordUnlinks Discord and stops Discord messages.Asks you first
